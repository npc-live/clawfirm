## Executive Summary

Anthropic has executed one of the fastest private-market valuation trajectories in venture history: from $61.5 B (March 2025) → $183 B (September 2025) → $350 B (November 2025) → **$380 B** (February 2026 Series G). Revenue is now tracking at **~$19 B ARR** (March 2026), growing >10× YoY for the third consecutive year.

Institutional access paths have materially expanded. As of March 2026, exposure is available via: (1) direct secondary market transactions, (2) SPV/fund structures including a newly exchange-listed closed-end fund, (3) tokenized on-chain instruments, (4) public equity proxies with balance-sheet stakes, and (5) AI-focused ETFs with direct or indirect holdings. Each path carries distinct risk/liquidity/regulatory profiles requiring careful due diligence.

**Primary signal:** Anthropic is the only frontier AI model deployed across all three hyperscale cloud platforms (AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, Microsoft Azure Foundry). This distribution moat is difficult to replicate and positions it as systemic AI infrastructure.

## 5. Structural Observations for Portfolio Construction

1. **Asymmetric leverage via AMZN/GOOGL:** Both companies have already recognized multi-billion-dollar P&L impact from Anthropic mark-ups. An IPO at $380 B+ would generate further realized gains, providing a direct EPS catalyst. This path offers the highest liquidity-adjusted Anthropic beta.

2. **The RWA tokenization pathway is nascent but maturing:** The $1 B+ tokenized equity market is growing rapidly. xStocks' 24% market share and $25 B cumulative volume signal institutional interest, but single-asset token liquidity (7,714 ANTHROPIC tokens total) remains inadequate for institutional block trades.

3. **VCX warrants a watchlist, not a position:** The NYSE listing is structurally interesting as the first exchange-traded vehicle with direct Anthropic equity, but the regulatory overhang and volatility profile make it unsuitable until the SEC review resolves. If cleared, re-rating potential is significant.

4. **The cloud compute circularity:** Anthropic's $80 B+ committed cloud spend flows back to AMZN, GOOGL, and MSFT. Investors in those companies benefit both from equity appreciation *and* from Anthropic as a cloud revenue driver — a compounding effect not visible in simple stake analysis.

5. **Cash burn trajectory:** $3 B/year burn with $64 B raised implies >20 years of runway at current rates — but compute costs scale with usage, and model development cycles are capital-intensive. Break-even in 2028 is management guidance; stress-test assumptions before modeling terminal value.
