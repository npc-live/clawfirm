# GTM Strategy Overview

## The core GTM question

Before picking any channel: **Who is your early customer and where do they already hang out?**

Your GTM strategy should answer:
1. **Who** — ICP (Ideal Customer Profile): who gets the most value from your product?
2. **Where** — Where do they discover tools like yours?
3. **Why now** — What's the trigger that makes them look for a solution?
4. **Message** — What's the one thing that makes them stop and pay attention?

---

## Stage-based GTM playbook

### Stage 1: Pre-launch / 0 users
**Goal:** Validate demand before spending months building

Tactics to run in parallel:
- [ ] Define your ICP (1 paragraph: job title, company size, pain, trigger)
- [ ] Identify 3–5 communities where your ICP hangs out
- [ ] Talk to 10–20 potential users before launch (see `channels/idea-validation.md`)
- [ ] Build a landing page that captures emails (see `channels/landing-pages-messaging.md`)
- [ ] Post a "I'm building X for Y, would this be useful?" on relevant communities

### Stage 2: Launch / 0 → 10 users
**Goal:** Get real users, not vanity metrics

Tactics (do all of these):
- [ ] Post on launch platforms: ProductHunt, Betalist, Uneed, HN Show (see `channels/launch-platforms.md`)
- [ ] Submit to 10–15 software directories for baseline traffic (see `channels/launch-platforms.md`)
- [ ] Manual outreach to ICP: LinkedIn, cold email, Reddit DMs (see `channels/cold-outreach.md`)
- [ ] Post on relevant subreddits (see `channels/reddit.md`)
- [ ] Build in public on X/LinkedIn (see `channels/social-media.md`)

### Stage 3: Early traction / 10 → 100 users
**Goal:** Find 1–2 repeatable channels

Pick 2 channels from this list and go deep for 60 days:
- Reddit / communities (if your ICP is active there)
- Cold outreach (if B2B)
- Content marketing (if you can produce original insights)
- SEO (if high search volume + you can create good content)
- Free tool (if you can build something small that's valuable alone)

Measure: signups per channel, conversion rate, CAC (even roughly).

### Stage 4: Scaling / 100 → 1000 users
**Goal:** Systematize what works, add new channels

- Double down on the 1–2 channels that produced results
- Start SEO if not already (it takes 3–6 months to compound)
- Consider affiliate/referral program (see `channels/affiliates-referrals.md`)
- Test paid ads once you have a clear landing page conversion rate (see `channels/ads.md`)
- Build in public consistently (compound over 6–12 months)

## Channel selection matrix

| ICP type | Top 3 channels | Avoid early |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| B2B SaaS (SMBs) | Cold email, LinkedIn, content | Paid ads, influencers |
| B2B dev tools | HN, dev communities, GitHub, content | Facebook ads |
| Consumer app | TikTok/Instagram, ProductHunt, ASO | Cold email |
| Productivity / indie | Reddit, ProductHunt, Twitter/X BIP | LinkedIn ads |
| AI tools | HN, ProductHunt, LLM SEO, Reddit | Most paid channels |
| No-code / low-code | ProductHunt, YouTube, Twitter/X | Cold email |
| Vertical SaaS (niche B2B) | Cold email, niche communities, LinkedIn | Broad content |

## The ICP framework

A useful ICP definition for early-stage has 5 elements:

1. **Who they are**: Job title / type of person
2. **What they're doing**: The specific situation or workflow where the problem occurs
3. **The pain**: What's frustrating or costing them time/money
4. **The trigger**: What event makes them start looking for a solution
5. **Why your product**: The specific reason they'd choose you over alternatives

**Example:**
> "Early-stage SaaS founders (1–5 person team) who are doing manual customer support in their inbox, frustrated by losing track of conversations and spending 2+ hours/day on support, often triggered by getting their first 50+ users. They'd choose us because we're simpler to set up than Intercom and 10x cheaper."

## GTM anti-patterns to avoid

- **Premature optimization**: Optimizing a channel before it's proven to produce users
- **Channel FOMO**: Doing 10 channels at 10% effort instead of 1 channel at 100%
- **Vanity metrics**: Optimizing for Twitter followers or Reddit karma instead of signups
- **Spray-and-pray outreach**: Sending 1000 cold emails without validating the message first with 20
- **Launch-and-forget**: Launching on ProductHunt once and expecting traffic forever
- **Building in private**: Not talking about what you're building until it's "ready"
