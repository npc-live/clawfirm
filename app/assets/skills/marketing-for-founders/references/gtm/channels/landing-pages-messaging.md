# 🎯 Landing Pages, Messaging & Positioning

## Why this matters

Most startup websites fail at the most basic thing: telling visitors who the product is for, what it does, and why it's better. This causes confused visitors, high bounce rates, and wasted marketing spend.

Your website is a sales and marketing asset. Every visitor who doesn't convert is money left on the table.

**The SaaS homepage structure that converts:**
1. **Hero**: Who it's for + what it does + CTA (above the fold)
2. **Social proof**: Logos or testimonials (immediately after hero)
3. **Problem section**: Describe the pain in the user's language
4. **Solution**: How your product solves it (features with context, not just feature names)
5. **More social proof**: Testimonials, case studies, metrics
6. **CTA section**: Sign up / start free trial / book demo

## Headlines & taglines

- [SaaS Headline Playbook](https://nicolasmerouze.notion.site/SaaS-Headline-Playbook-176830adbaa5477994946a3106473aba)

**Headline formula:**
> [Result/Outcome] for [ICP] without [main pain/friction]

**Examples:**
- "Ship production-ready APIs in minutes, not months" (dev tool)
- "Turn customer interviews into product decisions, automatically" (user research tool)
- "The invoicing app that doesn't require an accountant" (SMB finance)

**Common headline mistakes:**
- Too abstract ("The future of work")
- Too technical (only insiders understand)
- Feature-focused instead of outcome-focused ("AI-powered X" instead of "Never do X manually again")

## Social proof

- [Use Social Proof To Elevate Your GTM Efforts](https://knowledge.gtmstrategist.com/p/use-social-proof-to-elevate-gtm-strategy)
- [The Ultimate Guide on Case Studies & Testimonials](https://www.mrrunlocked.com/p/guide-case-studies)

**Types of social proof (by conversion power):**
1. Specific metrics: "Reduced our deployment time from 2 days to 15 minutes"
2. Named testimonials with company/role
3. Logo wall (company names your ICP recognizes)
4. Number of users/customers (only if impressive: >100 companies, >10,000 users)
5. Review platform badges (G2, Capterra)

## Quick audit checklist

Read your homepage as a first-time visitor who knows nothing about you:

- [ ] Within 5 seconds, can you tell who the product is for?
- [ ] Within 10 seconds, can you tell what it actually does?
- [ ] Is there a clear CTA above the fold?
- [ ] Does the page have at least one piece of social proof?
- [ ] Are there any jargon words a customer wouldn't use?
- [ ] Does the pricing page show the value, not just the price?
