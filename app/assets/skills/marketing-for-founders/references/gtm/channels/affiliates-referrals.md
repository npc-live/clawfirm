# 💑 Affiliates & Referrals

## When to use this channel

Word-of-mouth is the #1 growth driver for most bootstrapped startups. Affiliate and referral programs systematize and incentivize it.

**Why it works:**
- You only pay when it converts (zero CAC risk)
- Referred users trust the product more (social proof)
- Compounds over time — each new user can become a referrer

**Start when:** You have at least 50–100 happy users. Don't build a referral program when you have 10 users — there's not enough fuel.

---

## Referral programs (user-to-user)

Your existing users refer their friends/colleagues in exchange for rewards.

- [10 steps for a great Referral Program](https://thegrowthmind.substack.com/p/10-steps-for-a-great-referral-program)
- [Your guide to B2B referrals](https://web.archive.org/web/20250523213447/https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/your-guide-to-b2b-referrals)
- [8 Affiliate & Referral Email Examples](https://pin.it/5BxRb4Zv4)

**Referral reward options:**
- **SaaS credit** (cheapest for you): "Give 1 month free, get 1 month free" — Dropbox-style
- **Cash** (higher conversion): $X for every paying customer referred
- **Feature unlock**: Refer 3 friends to unlock [premium feature]
- **Dual-sided**: Both referrer and referred get a reward (Uber-style)

**For B2B SaaS:** Credit-based or cash works better than feature unlocks.

## Affiliate programs (partner-to-you)

Third-party partners (bloggers, newsletter writers, tool reviewers) promote you and get a commission.

- [Your ultimate guide to affiliate referrals for B2B SaaS](https://www.mrrunlocked.com/p/your-ultimate-guide-to-affiliate-referrals)

**Commission structures:**
- **Recurring:** 20–30% of MRR for as long as the customer stays (best for SaaS)
- **One-time:** 30–50% of first payment (easier to track, more motivating upfront)
- **Hybrid:** 30% for 12 months, then lower

**Where to find affiliates:**
- Your existing happy users (turn power users into affiliates)
- Bloggers and YouTubers who've reviewed similar tools
- Newsletter writers in your niche
- "Best of" and "alternatives" listicle writers

## Tools for referral/affiliate programs

**Simple referral (built-in):**
- Rewardful — easy to set up for SaaS
- Tolt — modern, simple
- FirstPromoter

**More sophisticated:**
- PartnerStack — B2B partnerships
- Impact.com — enterprise affiliates

**Manual (if you're early):** Just use a spreadsheet + unique promo codes. No need to pay for software until you have 10+ active affiliates.

## When referrals don't work

Common failure modes:
- **Reward too small**: $5 credit isn't motivating. Make the reward feel meaningful.
- **Too much friction**: Sharing should be one click, not a form to fill out.
- **Product not good enough**: Referrals only work if users love the product. Fix retention first.
- **Wrong user type**: B2C users refer freely; B2B users only refer if their reputation is on the line — make it easy for them to look good.
