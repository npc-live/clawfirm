# 🧲 Content Marketing

## When to use this channel

Content marketing is about creating content that attracts your ICP — not by talking about your product, but by teaching, entertaining, or helping them with their problems.

It takes time (3–6 months to see results) but compounds: good content gets traffic, backlinks, and social shares for years.

**Best for:** Founders who can produce genuine insights, have interesting learnings to share, or are building in a space with clear educational demand.

## Content distribution (the underrated part)

Writing the content is 30% of the work. Distribution is 70%.

- [How I promote my content — Marketing Examples](https://marketingexamples.com/content/sharing)
- [Content distribution checklist by Ross Simmonds](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XaqKmHnMcRRznR-JQJsktlzkZ2fRmszGWZTeLeRyYZk/edit?gid=210200339#gid=210200339)

**Distribution channels per content type:**

| Content type | Where to share |
|-------------|----------------|
| Blog post | Twitter/X, LinkedIn, relevant subreddits, HN, newsletters |
| Research / data study | LinkedIn, Twitter/X, journalists, newsletter roundups |
| Tutorial / how-to | Dev communities, Reddit, YouTube (short), newsletters |
| Case study | Cold email to similar companies, LinkedIn, Twitter/X |
| Video | YouTube, TikTok (repurpose), LinkedIn native video |

## Lead magnets

Free resources that capture email signups. Way more effective than "sign up for our newsletter."

- [How to create powerful SaaS Lead Magnets + examples](https://www.mrrunlocked.com/p/saas-lead-magnets)

**Lead magnet ideas:**
- Checklist or template (1-2 hours to make, high conversion)
- Mini email course (5-day series on a topic your ICP cares about)
- Free tool (see `channels/free-tool-marketing.md`)
- ROI calculator
- Curated resource list (like this repo!)

Getting written about in publications your ICP reads:

**Low-effort PR tactics for early-stage:**
- "We built X in public" posts (BuildInPublic community loves these)

- "We crossed X milestone" announcements
- Controversial takes backed by data
- Response articles to recent industry news

## Content cadence recommendation

Don't try to publish 3x/week. Publish 1 genuinely great piece per month > 12 mediocre ones.

**Month 1:** Write 1 definitive guide on your core problem (the article you wish existed when you had this problem)

**Month 2:** Write 1 case study or data story from your early users

**Month 3:** Write 1 "comparison" or "alternatives" piece targeting competitor keywords

Then distribute each piece aggressively across all relevant channels.
