# 💸 Ads

## When to use this channel

**Don't run ads before you have PMF.** Ads amplify what already works — they don't fix a broken funnel or unproven product.

Start ads when:
- You have a landing page converting at >2% (ideally >5%)
- You know your ICP clearly
- You have a clear value proposition
- You've validated messaging through organic channels
- You have budget to burn while learning (minimum $500–1000/month to get meaningful data)

---

## Google Ads

Best for: Intent-based demand (people already searching for your solution)

- [How to get started with Google Ads for early-stage B2B SaaS](https://www.mrrunlocked.com/p/google-ads-guide)

**Starter strategy:**
1. Start with exact-match keywords only (not broad)
2. Target bottom-of-funnel terms: "[category] software", "[competitor] alternative"
3. Send traffic to a dedicated landing page (not your homepage)
4. Budget: $20–30/day minimum to get learning data

## Reddit Ads

Underrated channel. Cheaper than LinkedIn, highly targeted by community.

- [How to run effective Reddit ads: Meme-Style, Incentive-First Copy, and "Is This an Ad?" Text Posts](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aayildirim_if-youre-running-reddit-ads-you-know-its-activity-7364309579888771073-tvTR)

**What works on Reddit:**
- Ads that look like organic posts ("Is This an Ad?" format)
- Meme-style visuals
- Community-specific targeting
- Offering something free first

## Key metrics to track

| Metric | What it tells you |
|--------|------------------|
| CTR (click-through rate) | Whether the ad creative/copy is compelling |
| CPC (cost per click) | Efficiency of spend |
| CVR (conversion rate) | Whether landing page works |
| CAC (customer acquisition cost) | Total cost to acquire 1 customer |
| LTV:CAC ratio | Sustainability (target >3:1) |

---

## Early-stage ad budget recommendation

- Month 1: $300–500 to test 3–5 creative variants (just learning)
- Month 2: Double down on the 1–2 variants that worked
- Month 3+: Scale budget if CAC is within acceptable range

If you've spent $500 and have zero signups, the issue is the funnel (landing page, offer, targeting) — not the budget.
