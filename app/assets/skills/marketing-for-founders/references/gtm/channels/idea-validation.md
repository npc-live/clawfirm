# 💡 Idea Validation

## Why this matters

The most painful (and common) startup mistake: spending 6 months building a product nobody wants. Validate demand in days or weeks, not months.

**The rule:** Talk to at least 10 potential users before writing a single line of code.

## Finding people with the problem (using Reddit)

Reddit is the best free tool for idea validation:

- [A four-part guide to finding problems your customers will pay you to solve using Reddit](https://shavinpeiries.com/scratch-their-itch/)

**How:**
1. Search Reddit for complaints about the problem you're solving
2. Find threads where people are already asking for a solution
3. DM people who posted about the problem: "Hey, I saw your post about X. I'm exploring building a solution. Would you be open to a 15-min call?"

If you can't find anyone complaining about this problem on Reddit, that's a red flag.

## Customer discovery interviews

- [Interview Script: Discovery Phase (before you've built anything)](https://deployempathy.substack.com/p/customer-interview-script-discovery-phase-i-e-before-you-ve-built-anything-415336)

**What to ask (discovery interview):**
- "Tell me about the last time you dealt with [problem]"
- "How are you solving it currently?"
- "How much time/money does that cost you?"
- "What would the ideal solution look like?"
- "Have you paid for anything to solve this?"

**What NOT to ask:**
- "Would you use this?" (people say yes to be nice)
- "Do you think this is a good idea?" (not useful)
- Questions about the future ("Would you pay for X?")

**The Mom Test rule:** If you describe your product and someone says "that sounds great!" — that's not validation. Validation is when they say "I need this right now, how do I pay?"

## Smoke tests (validate before building)

Ways to test demand without building the product:

1. **Landing page + waitlist:** Set up a Carrd/Framer page in a day, describe the product, collect emails. If 50+ people sign up in a week, there's interest.

2. **Fake door test:** Add a "Pricing" or "Sign Up" button. When people click, show "Coming soon — join the waitlist." Track click rate.

3. **Pre-sell:** Offer to build the product for someone who's willing to pay upfront (consulting-style). If nobody will pre-pay, reconsider.

4. **Sell manually first:** Do the thing manually for 5 customers before automating it. You'll learn 10x more.

5. **Cold email the offer:** Write a cold email describing the product to 20 perfect ICP targets. Track reply rate and interest.

## Validation signals (what actually counts)

| Signal | Strength |
|--------|----------|
| Someone pays upfront | ✅✅✅ Very strong |
| Someone refers others before launch | ✅✅✅ Very strong |
| 3+ people ask "when can I start using this?" | ✅✅ Strong |
| 20%+ email open rate on launch announcement | ✅✅ Strong |
| 5+ people join waitlist organically | ✅ Moderate |
| People say "great idea!" in interviews | ❌ Weak (not validation) |
| People say they "would probably" use it | ❌ Weak |
