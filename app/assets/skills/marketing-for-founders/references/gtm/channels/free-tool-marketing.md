# 🛠️ Free-Tool Marketing (Engineering as Marketing)

## What this is

Build a small, standalone free tool that solves one specific problem for your ICP — and use it to drive traffic, backlinks, and signups to your main product.

Also called "Engineering as Marketing" — using your technical skills as a marketing asset.

**Why it works:**
- Viral potential: people share useful tools
- SEO: tools get backlinks from "best free tools for X" articles
- Lead generation: free tool users convert to paid product users
- No ad spend needed

## Real examples that worked

- **Hubspot's Website Grader** — grade your website's performance, capture email
- **Ahrefs' Backlink Checker** — free limited version of their core product
- **Shopify's Logo Maker** — free for e-commerce founders, Shopify's ICP
- **Stripe's Revenue Calculator** — useful for anyone thinking about SaaS pricing
- **Buffer's Social Media Image Resizer** — used by exactly who Buffer wants as customers

**Pattern:** The free tool serves the same audience as the paid product, solves an adjacent problem, and makes users aware of the bigger solution.

## How to choose what to build

Good free tool criteria:
1. **Useful standalone** — works without signing up for your main product
2. **Relevant ICP** — used by exactly the people who might buy your main product
3. **Naturally leads to your product** — "want the full version / more features?"
4. **Shareable** — people want to show results to colleagues or friends
5. **Low maintenance** — set it and forget it (mostly)

**Brainstorm framework:**
- What's one calculation/analysis/generation your users do manually that you could automate?
- What's a simplified version of your main product's core value?
- What tool would your ICP Google for?

## Building tips

- Build in a weekend — keep it simple
- Make results shareable (screenshot-friendly, share button)
- Require email only if you must — frictionless is better for virality
- Add a subtle CTA to your main product at the end of the result
- Submit the tool to Product Hunt as its own product
- Submit to directories (Product Hunt, Uneed, etc.) — see `launch-platforms.md`

---

## Launch and distribution

1. Launch on ProductHunt separately from your main product
2. Post on relevant subreddits (people love genuinely useful free tools)
3. Write a blog post explaining how you built it
4. Submit to "best free tools for X" directories
5. Email your list about it
6. Reach out to newsletter writers in your niche — free tools are great newsletter content
