# 💌 Email Marketing

## When to use this channel

Email is your **only owned channel** — you own the list, no algorithm can take it away. It's essential for:
- Onboarding new users (getting them to the "aha moment")
- Boosting free-to-paid conversions
- Reducing churn by re-engaging inactive users
- Promoting affiliate/referral programs
- Collecting feedback at scale

Start building your email list from day 0. Even before you launch.

## Onboarding emails (highest leverage)

The onboarding sequence is the most important email series you'll write. Its job: get new users to the "aha moment" before they give up.

- [How to Build High-Converting SaaS Email Onboarding Sequences](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaaS/comments/1b7l04k/how_to_build_highconverting_saas_email_onboarding/)
- [Lifecycle email planning worksheets](https://userlist.com/worksheets/)
- [Onboarding Email concepts (25 Figma concepts)](https://www.figma.com/community/file/1207279330099591674/onboarding-emails-25-concepts)
- [How to optimize an email automation](https://copyhackers.com/2025/03/how-to-optimize-an-email/)

### 5-email onboarding sequence template

| Email | Timing | Goal |
|-------|--------|------|
| 1. Welcome | Immediately | Set expectations, show 1 first action |
| 2. Feature highlight | Day 2 | Show the core value, not all features |
| 3. Social proof | Day 4 | Case study / customer result |
| 4. Nudge | Day 7 | "Haven't done X yet?" — activate inactive users |
| 5. Check-in | Day 14 | Feedback request / offer help |

## Email deliverability basics

If your emails aren't reaching inboxes, nothing else matters:
- Set up SPF, DKIM, DMARC on your domain
- Use a subdomain for marketing email (e.g., mail.yourdomain.com)
- Warm up new email domains gradually
- Clean your list regularly (remove bounces and unengaged users)
- Never buy email lists
