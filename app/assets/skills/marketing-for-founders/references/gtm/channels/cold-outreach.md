# ❄️ Sales & Cold Outreach

## When to use this channel

Cold outreach (email and DMs) is the **most direct path to early users and revenue**. It's underrated because most people hate doing it — which means less competition.

Best for:
- B2B products (selling to businesses)
- Getting early feedback from target users
- Validating your ICP before building
- Finding your first 10 paying customers

Not ideal for:
- Consumer apps with a $5/month price point (unit economics don't work)
- Products with no clear ICP yet

---

## Before you start outreach

**Define your ICP first.** Cold outreach fails when you send to everyone. Send to 20 people with a precise ICP and iterate before sending to 1000.

## Cold email formula (for early-stage)

A working cold email structure:

```
Subject: [Specific pain] at [Company type]?

Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific observation about them — company, recent news, role].

We built [product] for [ICP] who are struggling with [pain].

[One-line result]: [Customer type] using us typically [specific outcome].

Worth a 15-min call to see if it fits?

[Name]
```

**Rules:**
- No more than 5 sentences in the body
- One CTA only (a call, a trial, a reply — pick one)
- Personalize at least the first line (not "Hi {First Name}")
- Follow up 2–3 times spaced 4–7 days apart — most replies come from follow-ups

## Metrics to track

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Open rate | >40% (subject line problem if lower) |
| Reply rate | >5% (message problem if lower) |
| Positive reply rate | >2% (targeting problem if lower) |
| Demo booked rate | >1% of total sent |
