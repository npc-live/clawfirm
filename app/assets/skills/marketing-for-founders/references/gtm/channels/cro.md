# 🔥 Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

## What this is

CRO is about improving the percentage of visitors who take your desired action (sign up, upgrade, book a demo). Small improvements compound: doubling your conversion rate is equivalent to doubling your traffic — for free.

**Start CRO when:** You have at least 500–1000 monthly visitors. With less traffic, A/B test results are statistically unreliable.

## High-impact CRO experiments to run

### CTAs (Call to Action)

- [The most powerful CTA?](https://www.marketingideas.com/p/the-most-powerful-cta)

**Test order (by typical impact):**
1. CTA text: "Start free trial" vs "Get started free" vs "Try for free"
2. CTA placement: above the fold vs. sticky header vs. end of page
3. CTA color and contrast
4. CTA size

**What tends to work:**
- Outcome-focused CTAs: "Start growing today" > "Sign up"
- Free trial or freemium CTA > "Book a demo" (for self-serve products)
- Reduce friction: no credit card required

## CRO without A/B testing (qualitative methods)

For early-stage (<1000 visitors/month), skip A/B tests — not enough statistical power. Use qualitative methods instead:

1. **Session recording** (Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity — free): Watch real users navigate your site. Where do they stop? What do they click?

2. **Heatmaps**: See where users scroll, click, and lose interest.

3. **5-second test**: Show your homepage to someone unfamiliar for 5 seconds, then ask what they remember. What the product does should be clear.

4. **User interviews**: Ask 5 users to narrate their experience signing up. Where were they confused?

5. **Exit intent survey**: Pop up a 1-question survey when users are about to leave: "What stopped you from signing up today?"

## Common conversion killers

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Unclear hero message | Rewrite to say exactly who it's for and what it does |
| No social proof above the fold | Add a testimonial or logo bar immediately after hero |
| "Book a Demo" as primary CTA (for self-serve) | Add a free trial CTA |
| Required credit card on free trial | Remove it |
| 10-step signup form | Cut to 3 fields maximum |
| No mobile optimization | Fix it — 50%+ traffic is mobile |
| Slow page load | Compress images, use CDN |

## Metrics to track

| Metric | What it measures |
|--------|-----------------|
| Homepage → signup conversion rate | Homepage effectiveness |
| Signup → activation rate | Onboarding effectiveness |
| Free → paid conversion rate | Product value + pricing |
| Trial → paid conversion rate | Trial experience |
| Churn rate | Retention and product fit |

**Benchmarks for SaaS:**
- Homepage conversion: 2–5% good, >5% great
- Free-to-paid: 2–5% for freemium, 15–30% for trials
