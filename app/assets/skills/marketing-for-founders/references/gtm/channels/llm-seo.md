# 🤖 LLM SEO / AEO / GEO

## What this is

**AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** and **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — the practice of getting your product recommended by AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.

This is one of the highest-leverage new channels in 2025. Unlike traditional SEO, you can start getting AI citations within days, not months.

---

## Why it matters

More and more users ask AI chatbots "what's the best tool for X?" instead of Googling. If your product shows up in those answers, you get high-intent traffic from people already looking for exactly what you offer.

> "Tally turned AI into their top acquisition source" — and they weren't trying to do AI SEO, they just had good content and strong brand presence.

## How AI engines decide what to recommend

AI models are trained on internet data. They recommend products that:
1. Appear frequently in trustworthy sources (reviews, comparisons, articles)
2. Are mentioned in the context of solving specific problems
3. Have strong brand presence (real users talking about them)
4. Have clear, structured information about what they do

**Key insight:** Getting mentioned on G2, Capterra, Reddit, and niche blogs matters more than your own website content for AI recommendations.

---

## Practical tactics

**Start here (quick wins):**
- [ ] Get listed on G2, Capterra, Trustpilot — AI engines trust review platforms
- [ ] Get listed on "best of" and "alternatives to" pages in your category
- [ ] Create a clear `/llms.txt` or `robots.txt` that doesn't block AI crawlers
- [ ] Write comparison pages: "[Your product] vs [Competitor]"
- [ ] Create "best [category] tools" content where you include yourself

**Medium term:**
- [ ] Encourage users to mention your product on Reddit and communities (natural mentions)
- [ ] Get mentions in newsletters and blogs in your niche
- [ ] Create genuinely useful content that other sites link to

**Advanced:**
- [ ] Submit to AI-specific directories: [There's an AI for that](https://theresanaiforthat.com/), [FutureTools](https://www.futuretools.io/), etc.
- [ ] Create structured data (schema.org) on your site

## Metrics to track

- What does ChatGPT say when asked "best [category] tools"?
- What does Claude say?
- What does Perplexity say?

Run these queries monthly and track your position. Getting from "not mentioned" to "mentioned" is the first goal. Getting to "recommended first" is the end goal.
