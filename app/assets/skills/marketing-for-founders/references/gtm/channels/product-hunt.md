# 😾 Product Hunt Launch

## When to use this channel

Product Hunt is still one of the most effective ways to get early users — if you execute well. A successful launch (#1 or top 5 of the day) can bring:
- 500–5000+ signups in a day
- Press coverage and backlinks
- Credibility and social proof
- Feedback from early adopters

**The catch:** It takes serious preparation (2–4 weeks minimum), promotion on launch day, and follow-up work. A lazy launch gets you nothing.

## Key success factors

**Before launch:**
- Build a hunter network: get 200+ people who will upvote on launch day (collect emails/contacts)
- Prepare your assets: gallery screenshots, demo video (60 sec max), tagline
- Schedule for Tuesday–Thursday (highest traffic days)
- Post at 12:01 AM PST (to maximize time at top)
- Prep your community: let your email list, Twitter, LinkedIn followers know

**The tagline formula:**
> [Product name] — [What it does] for [who] without [the main pain]

**Thumbnail:** Use a clean, simple visual. Text should be readable at small size.

**Launch day:**
- Be online all day to respond to every comment
- Ask for honest feedback, not just upvotes
- Share the link to your launch on every channel (email, social, Slack groups, communities)
- DM people individually (not spam — targeted people who'd genuinely care)

**After launch:**
- Respond to all comments for 2–3 days
- Follow up with people who showed interest
- Write a post-mortem / "we launched" post for your audience

## What to prepare

- [ ] Product tagline (≤60 chars)
- [ ] Product description (2–3 paragraphs)
- [ ] 5–10 gallery screenshots or GIFs
- [ ] 60-second demo video
- [ ] First comment from the maker (your personal story)
- [ ] List of 200+ people to notify on launch day
- [ ] Email to your existing list
- [ ] Social media posts scheduled for launch morning

---

## Common mistakes

- Launching on Monday or Friday (low traffic days)
- No prep / no hunter network
- Responding defensively to critical comments
- Not engaging with the community during the day
- Launching with a broken onboarding flow (people will try it and leave)
