# 📷 Social Media Marketing

## When to use this channel

Social media for founders is primarily two tactics: **Building in Public** (your personal brand) and **Social Listening** (finding conversations to join). Both are free, both compound over time, and both work best when done consistently over 6–12 months.

Don't try to manage every platform. Pick one (LinkedIn for B2B, X/Twitter for tech/indie, TikTok for consumer) and go deep.

---

## Building in Public

Share your journey, expertise, and product updates on your personal profile. This is founder-led marketing — you're the brand, not the product.

**Why it works:** People buy from people they trust. Sharing your process builds trust before someone needs your product.

**What to post:**
- Product milestones (first customer, MRR numbers, feature launches)
- Behind-the-scenes decisions (why you chose X over Y)
- Learnings from failures
- Insights from talking to customers
- Controversial takes in your domain
- Genuine questions to your audience

**What NOT to post:**
- Pure promotional posts ("Check out my product!")
- Vague inspiration content with no substance
- Reposted content from others without your perspective

## Social Listening

Track conversations where your potential customers are already talking about their problems — and join them helpfully.

**How it works:**
1. Define 10–20 keywords related to your product's pain point
2. Monitor those keywords on Reddit, Twitter/X, LinkedIn
3. Join conversations where you can genuinely help (no spammy promotion)
4. Occasionally mention your product when highly relevant and asked

## Practical starting point

**Week 1:** Set up F5 bot with your 10 most important keywords. Start monitoring.

**Week 2:** Post 1 Building in Public post on your best platform. Could be: "I'm building X because of this problem I had. Here's what I've learned so far..."

**Month 1:** Post 3–4 times per week. Engage with 5–10 other people's posts per day (genuine comments, not "great post!").

**Measure:** Followers gained, replies/DMs from potential users, inbound signups from social.
